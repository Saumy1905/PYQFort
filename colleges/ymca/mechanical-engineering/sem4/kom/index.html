--- layout: subject title: Kinematics of Machines - Semester 4 - Mechanical Engineering subject_name: Kinematics of Machines subject_slug: kom college_name: JC Bose University college_slug: ymca branch_name: Mechanical Engineering branch_slug: mechanical-engineering semester_name: Semester 4 semester_slug: sem4 semester_number: 4 code: PCC-ME-403-21 description: Study of the motion of machines and mechanisms, focusing on their kinematic analysis and design. permalink: /colleges/ymca/mechanical-engineering/sem4/kom/ subject_content: | ## Kinematics of Machines **Definition**: Kinematics of Machines focuses on the geometric aspects of motion in mechanisms, including velocity, acceleration, and profile synthesis without considering forces. ### **Unit 1: Mechanism and Machine** - Links, Kinematic pairs, Degree of freedom, Kinematic Chain. - Binary, Ternary, Quaternary Links and Joints. - Inversions of Mechanisms. - Application of Lower Pairs: Pantograph, Straight Line Mechanisms. - Approximate Straight Line Motion Mechanism. - Steering gears: Davis Steering gear, Ackermann Steering gear. - Universal Hook’s Joint. ### **Unit 2: Motion Analysis in Mechanism** - Concept of Instantaneous centre method to analyze Velocity in Simple Mechanism. - Method for locating an instantaneous centre. - Relative Velocity method to analyze Velocity and acceleration. - Rubbing velocity at pin joints. - Coriolis acceleration component. ### **Unit 3: Synthesis of Mechanisms** - Kinematics synthesis of Mechanisms. - Type, number and dimensional synthesis. - Function generation, path generation and body guidance. - Two and three position synthesis of four bar and slider crank mechanisms by graphical methods. - Freudenstein’s equation, precision points. ### **Unit 4: Gears and Gear Trains** - Classification of gears, Gear terminology. - Law of gearing, Velocity of sliding. - Forms of teeth: cycloidal and involute profiles. - Interference and undercutting. - Minimum number of teeth on pinion and wheel to avoid interference. - Simple, compound, reverted and epicyclic gear trains. - Torque calculations in gear trains. ### **Unit 5: Cams and Followers** - Types of cams and followers. - Cam terminology, Motion of follower. - Uniform velocity, Simple harmonic motion, Uniform acceleration and retardation. - Construction of cam profiles. - Cam with specified contours. ### **Unit 6: Brakes and Dynamometers** - Introduction to brakes, Types of brakes. - Block brake, Band brake, Internal expanding shoe brake. - Braking torque calculations. - Introduction to dynamometers, Types: Prony brake, Rope brake, Belt transmission, Epicyclic and Bevis Gibson torsion dynamometers. --- ---
