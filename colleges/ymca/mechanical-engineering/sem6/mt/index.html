drill bushes, drilling jigs - fixture: turning fixture, milling fixture, grinding fixture ### **Unit 2: Powder metallurgy and Plastic manufacturing** - Powder Metallurgy - steps of powder metallurgy - compacting and sintering - applications of powder metallurgy - Plastics - Types of plastics - Injection moulding - application, merits and demerits - plastic extrusion ### **Unit 3: Press Tools and Design of Punch and Die** - press operations - types of presses - press working terminology - press rating - types of dies - design of die and punch - principles of forging die design ### **Unit 4: Metrology** - Dimensions, forms and surface measurements - Thermal metal removal process - Electric discharge machining - construction detail - principle - processes parameters - MRR - surface finish - tool wear - dielectric - power and control circuits - wire cut EDM - Plasma arc machining (PAM) - Laser beam machining (LBM) - Electron Beam principle, application and limitations ### **Unit 6: Non-conventional machining methods** - mechanical processes, electrochemical process: Abrasive Jet Machining, Water Jet Machining, Abrasive Water Jet Machining, and Ultrasonic Machining - Electro-chemical machining (ECM) - principle - elements of ECM - advantages, application and limitation - Chemical machining - principle - etchant and maskents - advantage, application and limitation --- ---
