Melanogaster, C. elegans, A. Thaliana, M. musculus. ### **Module 3: Genetics Purpose** - Mendel’s laws, segregation, independent assortment. - Concept of allele, gene mapping, gene interaction, epistasis. - Meiosis and mitosis (focus on genetic material transfer). - Recessiveness, dominance. - Mapping phenotype to genes. - Single gene disorders in humans. - Complementation using human genetics. ### **Module 4: Biomolecules Purpose** - Building blocks of life. - Monomeric units and polymeric structures. - Sugars, starch, cellulose. - Amino acids and proteins. - Nucleotides and DNA/RNA. - Two carbon units and lipids. ### **Module 5: Enzymes** - Enzyme classification, kinetics. - Mechanism of action, regulation. ### **Module 6: Information Transfer & Applications** - Central dogma: DNA to RNA to protein. - Biotechnology basics and applications. --- ## Computer Organization and Architecture **Definition**: Computer Organization and Architecture explores the structure, functioning, and design of computer systems from gates to processors. ### **Module 1: Digital Logic Basics** - Gates, Boolean algebra. - Combinational and sequential circuits. ### **Module 2: Register Transfer** - Register transfer language. - Microoperations. ### **Module 3: CPU Organization** - Instruction formats, addressing modes. - Control unit design. ### **Module 4: Memory System** - Hierarchy, cache mapping. - Virtual memory. ### **Module 5: I/O Organization** - Interrupts, DMA. - Bus systems. ### **Module 6: Pipelining** - Pipeline hazards. - Performance issues. --- ## Design and Analysis of Algorithms **Definition**: Design and Analysis of Algorithms teaches algorithmic paradigms, complexity analysis, and efficient problem-solving techniques. ### **Module 1: Introduction** - Algorithm complexity, asymptotic notation. - Recurrences. ### **Module 2: Divide and Conquer** - Merge sort, quick sort. - Matrix multiplication. ### **Module 3: Greedy Algorithms** - Activity selection, knapsack. - Huffman coding. ### **Module 4: Dynamic Programming** - 0/1 knapsack, LCS. - Matrix chain multiplication. ### **Module 5: Graph Algorithms** - BFS, DFS, shortest paths. - MST algorithms. ### **Module 6: Complexity Theory** - P, NP, NP-complete. - Approximation algorithms. --- ## Discrete Mathematics **Definition**: Discrete Mathematics provides mathematical foundations for computing, including logic, sets, graphs, and counting. ### **Module 1: Logic and Proofs** - Propositional logic, predicates. - Proof techniques. ### **Module 2: Sets and Relations** - Operations, functions. - Equivalence, partial orders. ### **Module 3: Counting** - Permutations, combinations. - Pigeonhole principle. ### **Module 4: Graph Theory** - Graphs, paths, trees. - Connectivity, coloring. ### **Module 5: Algebraic Structures** - Groups, rings basics. - Boolean algebra. ### **Module 6: Recurrences** - Solving recurrences. - Generating functions. --- ## Operating System **Definition**: Operating System studies resource management, process scheduling, memory allocation, and file systems in computing environments. ### **Module 1: Introduction** - OS functions, types. - System calls. ### **Module 2: Processes** - Process concept, scheduling. - Threads, IPC. ### **Module 3: Synchronization** - Critical section, semaphores. - Classic problems. ### **Module 4: Deadlocks** - Prevention, avoidance. - Detection. ### **Module 5: Memory Management** - Paging, segmentation. - Virtual memory, demand paging. ### **Module 6: File Systems** - File operations, directory. - Disk scheduling. --- ## Organizational Behaviour **Definition**: Organizational Behaviour examines individual and group dynamics in organizations to improve effectiveness and productivity. ### **Module 1: Introduction** - Concept, scope. - Models of OB. ### **Module 2: Individual Behaviour** - Personality, perception. - Attitudes, values. ### **Module 3: Motivation** - Theories: Maslow, Herzberg. - Contemporary approaches. ### **Module 4: Group Dynamics** - Teams, leadership. - Communication. ### **Module 5: Organizational Culture** - Power, politics. - Conflict management. ### **Module 6: Change Management** - Organizational development. - Stress management. --- ---
