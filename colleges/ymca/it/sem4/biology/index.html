Melanogaster, C. elegans, A. Thaliana, M. musculus. ### **Module 3: Genetics Purpose** - Mendel’s laws, segregation, independent assortment. - Concept of allele, gene mapping, gene interaction, epistasis. - Meiosis and mitosis (focus on genetic material transfer). - Recessiveness, dominance. - Mapping phenotype to genes. - Single gene disorders in humans. - Complementation using human genetics. ### **Module 4: Biomolecules Purpose** - Building blocks of life. - Monomeric units and polymeric structures. - Sugars, starch, cellulose. - Amino acids and proteins. - Nucleotides and DNA/RNA. - Two carbon units and lipids. ### **Module 5: Enzymes** - Enzyme classification, kinetics. - Mechanism of action, regulation. ### **Module 6: Information Transfer & Applications** - Central dogma: DNA to RNA to protein. - Biotechnology basics and applications. --- ---
