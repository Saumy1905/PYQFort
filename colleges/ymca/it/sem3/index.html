P-N junction diode, I-V characteristics. - Half-wave and full-wave rectifiers. - Zener diodes, clipping and clamping circuits. ### **Module 2: BJT Circuits** - BJT structure and I-V characteristics. - BJT as switch and amplifier, small-signal model. - Biasing circuits, current mirror. - Common-emitter, common-base, common-collector amplifiers. - High-frequency equivalent circuits. ### **Module 3: MOSFET Circuits** - MOSFET structure and I-V characteristics. - MOSFET as switch and amplifier, small-signal model. - Biasing circuits. - Common-source, common-gate, common-drain amplifiers. - Gain, impedances, transconductance, high-frequency model. ### **Module 4: Operational Amplifiers** - Differential amplifier. - Internal structure of op-amp, ideal and non-ideal characteristics. - Offset voltage, bias current, slew rate, gain bandwidth product. ### **Module 5: Linear Op-Amp Applications** - Inverting/non-inverting amplifiers, instrumentation amplifier. - Integrator, differentiator, active filters. - Oscillators (Wein bridge, phase shift). ### **Module 6: Nonlinear Op-Amp Applications** - Comparators, precision rectifier. - Waveform generators, peak detector. --- ## Calculus and Ordinary Differential Equations **Definition**: Calculus and Ordinary Differential Equations covers multivariable calculus, multiple integrals, vector calculus, and methods for solving differential equations. ### **Module 1: Multivariable Calculus** - Partial derivatives, chain rule. - Maxima/minima, Lagrange multipliers. ### **Module 2: Multiple Integrals** - Double and triple integrals. - Change of variables, applications. ### **Module 3: Vector Calculus** - Gradient, divergence, curl. - Line, surface, volume integrals; theorems. ### **Module 4: First-Order ODEs** - Exact, linear, Bernoulli equations. - Applications. ### **Module 5: Higher-Order ODEs** - Linear with constant coefficients. - Variation of parameters, Cauchy-Euler. ### **Module 6: Series Solutions** - Power series method. - Special functions (Legendre, Bessel). --- ## Data Structures and Algorithms **Definition**: Data Structures and Algorithms teaches efficient data organization and algorithmic techniques for problem-solving with complexity analysis. ### **Module 1: Introduction** - Data structures overview. - Algorithm analysis, asymptotic notation. ### **Module 2: Arrays and Linked Lists** - Arrays, operations. - Singly, doubly, circular linked lists. ### **Module 3: Stacks and Queues** - Stack applications, expression evaluation. - Queues, priority queues, deque. ### **Module 4: Trees** - Binary trees, traversals. - Binary search trees, AVL trees. ### **Module 5: Graphs** - Representations, BFS, DFS. - Shortest paths. ### **Module 6: Sorting and Searching** - Insertion, selection, bubble, merge, quick sort. - Hashing. --- ## Digital Electronics **Definition**: Digital Electronics covers logic gates, combinational/sequential circuits, and design of digital systems using Boolean algebra. ### **Module 1: Number Systems & Codes** - Binary, octal, hexadecimal. - BCD, Gray, error detection codes. ### **Module 2: Boolean Algebra** - Laws, minimization (K-map, QM method). ### **Module 3: Combinational Circuits** - Adders, subtractors, multiplexers. - Decoders, encoders. ### **Module 4: Sequential Circuits** - Flip-flops (SR, JK, D, T). - Registers, counters. ### **Module 5: Memory & PLDs** - RAM, ROM. - PLA, PAL. ### **Module 6: Logic Families** - TTL, CMOS. - Interfacing. --- ## Economics for Engineers **Definition**: Economics for Engineers introduces micro and macro economic concepts relevant to engineering decisions, including demand, cost, markets, and financial analysis. ### **Module 1: Introduction** - Micro and Macro Economics. - Relationship between Science, Engineering, Technology and Economic Development. - Production Possibility Curve. - Nature of Economic Laws. ### **Module 2: Time Value of Money** - Concepts and application. - Capital budgeting: Payback period, IRR, ARR, NPV, PI (with case studies). ### **Module 3: Demand** - Law of Demand. - Elasticity of Demand: meaning, factors, practical application. - Demand forecasting. ### **Module 4: Production and Costs** - Factors of production. - Law of variable proportions, returns to scale. - Economies and diseconomies of scale. - Concepts of cost: accounting, opportunity, marginal. - Break even analysis, Make or Buy decision. - Relevance of Depreciation. ### **Module 5: Markets** - Types of market: perfect competition, monopoly, monopolistic, oligopoly. - Supply and law of supply. - Role of demand & supply in price determination. ### **Module 6: Indian Economy** - Nature and characteristics. - Fiscal and monetary policy. - LPG policy, Inflation, Sensex. - GATT, WTO, IMF. - Difference between Central bank and Commercial banks. --- ---
